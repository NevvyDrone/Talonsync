Talon Pilot Help

Quick fixes for Talon Pilot 2.19 and TalonSync

Try these first

  1. Turn on Self Interact in VRChat's settings (needed to tap your own screen).
  2. Open Tools › Talon Pilot › Install Wizard. The Overview shows a self-check; if it says Update or Repair, press it.
  3. Make sure TalonSync and Talon Pilot are the latest versions from your download.

Installing and updating

How do I install Talon Pilot?
  1. Import the Talon Pilot package into your avatar project (Unity 2022.3 with the VRChat Avatars SDK 3).
  2. Open Tools › Talon Pilot › Install Wizard, pick your avatar and follow the Guided Setup.
  3. Test in Play Mode, then upload. In VRChat: Expressions › Talon Pilot › Power.
How do I update to a new version?

Copy the new Assets/TalonPilot folder over the old one, open the Install Wizard and press Update. Your placement, settings, text and pet are kept. Tools › Talon Pilot › Patch Avatars to Latest updates every avatar in the scene at once.

Unity is stuck on "Compiling shader variants" or says the shader compiler timed out

The first import compiles the screen shader, which can take several minutes. That's normal and only happens once per set of options.

If Unity reports a timeout: choose Tools › Talon Pilot › Allow Long Shader Compiles, restart Unity and Unity Hub, then right-click Assets/TalonPilot and choose Reimport.

The upload fails: too many parameters or synced bits

Talon Pilot uses 9 to 11 of VRChat's 256 synced bits. If your avatar is already near the limit, free a few bits from other toggles, or leave out Beam and TalonStar (Control Center › Apps & Features › Apps on your PDA), which saves 2.

If VRChat rejects the avatar for having too many parameters overall, leave out apps you don't use and turn off Save typed text or Edit Calendar & Contacts on the PDA in Apps & Features.

How do I remove or repair Talon Pilot?

Repair: open the Install Wizard. If pieces are missing it offers Repair. Remove: Control Center › Support › Remove Talon Pilot from this avatar. Your FX controller is backed up first.

Using it in VRChat

I can't see the PDA
  • Switch it on: Expressions › Talon Pilot › Power.
  • Check that your avatar isn't blocked by a performance or safety setting (other players may need to show your avatar).
  • In Unity, run the wizard's self-check (Overview). If the PDA was dragged onto another bone, press Update to reconnect it.
The screen doesn't respond to my taps

Turn on Self Interact in VRChat's settings and tap with the very tip of the stylus. If you chose Finger in the wizard, use the index finger of your other hand.

The PDA or stylus is in the wrong place, or the stylus doesn't hide

Move or rotate them in the Hierarchy. To ride on a wing or paw, use Anchor points in the wizard (Placement). Don't drag them onto another bone by hand; if you did, press Update and they reconnect where you put them.

Owl Oracle doesn't react to shaking

Give the PDA a firm flick, or press Center or tap the screen instead. The shake sensor only runs while the Oracle is open, and only if the Oracle wasn't left out in the wizard.

Scope shows my voice, not the music

Scope's Spectrum and Waveform need AudioLink in the world. In worlds without AudioLink they switch to your voice, and the other styles (LED bars, Sunburst, Plasma and the rest) run from your voice too.

The Snow Globe doesn't stir when I shake it

It takes a hard shake, so normal movement doesn't set it off. You can also tap the globe or press Center.

The Terminal says "No CPU found"

Talon Enhanced BASIC runs on your PC inside TalonSync (1.10.1 or later) and is an experimental feature you opt in to: in TalonSync open Settings › Experimental and switch on Terminal CPU. Start TalonSync, then switch the PDA off and on (or reopen the Terminal) and the screen comes back with a menu: 1 loads Talon Enhanced BASIC, 2 opens the local terminal with its own commands (type HELP). The Terminal also needs PC link ticked in the Install Wizard.

I don't know BASIC. Can I still use the Terminal?

Yes. Choose 1 TalonOS Enhanced from the Terminal's start menu, open Programs and press Run. You can tap the screen or use the buttons (Up/Down choose, Center opens, Left goes back). There are games, a talking owl, a short BASIC tour and some demos. L shows how a program is written and E opens it in BASIC so you can change it.

Tapping TalonOS Enhanced does nothing

Taps reach TalonSync over OSC, so run Apply to avatar again with Talon Pilot 2.19 (it lists the touch grid for OSC), and if VRChat kept an old OSC config, reset it (Action Menu › Options › OSC › Reset Config). The buttons work either way.

The boot screen shows a warning or FAIL

Each boot line is a real check. First boot, set up is normal once on a new avatar. A line was held means something touched the screen while it started (a finger or the other hand). ROM FAIL means the ROM texture was changed or compressed: run Apply to avatar again. RAM FAIL means the animator didn't reach the screen: run Repair in the Control Center. PC link: none just means TalonSync wasn't running (it needs 1.8.7 or later).

The fish don't come when I tap

Tap inside the tank (above the bottom bar). The food drops where you tapped and the fish take a few seconds to swim over. Press Center to drop more in the same place.

An app is missing from the launcher

It was probably left out in the wizard. Open Control Center › Apps & Features › Apps on your PDA, tick it and press Apply to avatar.

I want to start over

Expressions › Talon Pilot › Reset has a factory reset that puts every preference back.

Desktop mode

On desktop I can't tap anything

Start TalonSync 1.7 or newer and press the middle mouse button while the dot in the middle of your screen is over the PDA (VRChat must be the window in front). Without TalonSync, use Expressions › Talon Pilot › Desktop › Tap where I look.

The PDA drifts off to the side on desktop

Use Expressions › Talon Pilot › Desktop › Bring PDA in front. It also comes back by itself when you walk off or turn away. Parking needs VRChat Avatars SDK 3.7 or newer.

TalonSync on your PC

TalonSync says it's waiting for VRChat or can't find the PDA
  1. Turn on OSC in VRChat: Action Menu › Options › OSC › Enabled.
  2. In TalonSync, run Settings › Connection › Connection doctor › Fix.
  3. Switch avatars once (VRChat sends its OSC list when an avatar loads).
  4. On Linux, try turning OSCQuery off in TalonSync's settings.
Windows warns about TalonSync.exe

Windows SmartScreen sometimes warns about new programs it hasn't seen often. Choose More info › Run anyway if you built or downloaded it from your Talon Pilot download. You can also run it without building: Run TalonSync (without building).bat.

TalonSync is missing features I've read about

You're probably running an older TalonSync.exe. Quit it from the tray and run Build TalonSync.bat again. The version is shown under the TalonSync name.

TalonStar says "No weather yet"

Start TalonSync on your computer and choose your city on its TalonStar page.

Performance

My frame rate is lower than I'd like
  • Switch the PDA off when you're not using it: almost all of its work stops.
  • Leave out apps you don't use (Control Center › Apps & Features › Apps on your PDA). Hoot-agotchi is the biggest saving.
  • If you never use VRChat on a desktop, turn off Desktop mode support (Control Center › Desktop).
  • Control Center › Performance shows the budget.

Getting help

Send your support info with your question

  1. In Unity: press Copy support info at the bottom of the Install Wizard (or Tools › Talon Pilot › Copy Support Info).
  2. In TalonSync: Settings › Connection › Copy support info.
  3. Paste both into your message with what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Support info holds versions, your options, the self-check and recent errors. Nothing personal.

Where to ask

Email nevvydrone@nevvydrone.com, or message me through the store where you bought Talon Pilot (Jinxxy).

Replies usually take 2 to 3 days. Support covers Talon Pilot and TalonSync themselves; I can't fix other avatars, worlds or tools, but I'll point you the right way.
